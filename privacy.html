Privacy Policy

Call Pilot · Effective September 20, 2026

Call Pilot provides an AI phone receptionist for small home-service businesses such as heating and air-conditioning companies. When you call one of those businesses and it does not answer, Call Pilot may answer the call for it. This policy explains what information Call Pilot collects when that happens and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Your phone number (caller ID) and the date, time and length of the call.
  • Details you give during the call: your name, service address, a description of the problem, and your preferred appointment time.
  • A recording and a written transcript of the call.
  • Whether you agreed to receive a text message confirmation, and any text messages you send back to us.

How we use it

  • To answer your call, book, change or cancel an appointment, and pass your request to the business you called.
  • To send you a text message confirming your appointment, but only if you agreed to it during the call.
  • To pass your text replies to the business you called.
  • To keep the service secure and working, and to meet legal obligations.

Who receives your information

The business you called receives your name, phone number, address, request, and the call recording and transcript. We also use service providers that handle information for us only to run the service: phone and text delivery, speech and language processing, calendar, and email. They may not use your information for their own purposes.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Mobile information and text message opt-in consent are never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Text messages

If you agree during the call, we send you one text message to confirm your appointment, and we pass on any replies you send. Message frequency is low, typically one text per booking. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts, or HELP for help. See our Terms & Conditions for details.

Recordings

Calls answered by Call Pilot are recorded and transcribed so the business you called has an accurate record of your request.

Keeping and deleting information

We keep call records for as long as needed to provide the service and to meet legal obligations. You can ask us to delete your information or tell you what we hold by emailing us at the address below.
